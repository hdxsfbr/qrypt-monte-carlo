One item per job (key JOB#<id> / META), written by store.put_job with attribute_not_exists(pk). Fields: status (QUEUED, COMPLETE, PARTIAL), params, shards, completed/failed counters, master_seed_hex, seed_source, algo_version, runtime, created_at/created_ts, gsi1pk/gsi1sk, optional fail_shards, replay_of, and on finish result, black_scholes, failed_shards, finished_at, replay_check, stale. Finished by store.finish_job, conditional on status QUEUED.
