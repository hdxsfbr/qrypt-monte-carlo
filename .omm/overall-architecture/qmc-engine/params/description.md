src/qmc/params.py. Defines Limits (max_paths 10M, max_shards 20, max_paths_per_shard 500k, fault_injection), JobParams (S0, K, r, sigma, T, paths, shards), JobRequest and ValidationError(field, message). validate_job_request rejects non-objects, unknown fields, missing fields, booleans, non-finite numbers, non-positive S0/K/sigma/T, |r| > 1, sigma > 5, T > 100, paths < 2, shards out of range or above paths, too many paths per shard, and fail_shards unless fault injection is enabled.
